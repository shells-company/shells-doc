title: "Summer Drift"
---

The heat has settled in like a houseguest.<br />Everything moves at its pace now.<br />The fan hums like it has something to say<br />and keeps forgetting the ending.<br /><br />I haven't gone out.<br />Not really.<br />The door stays shut,<br />and the windows glow amber at 7 PM<br />and I tell myself that's beautiful.<br /><br />I started a list of things I'd do this summer.<br />The list is still a title.<br />I wrote it three different ways.<br />Each one looked like a promise I couldn't keep.<br /><br />My phone is full of voice memos<br />I never replay.<br />Thoughts I entrusted to a machine<br />because I didn't trust the page.<br />Maybe I'll listen in the fall.<br />Maybe for this fall.<br /><br />I'm not behind.<br />I keep saying this like a spell.<br />The tomatoes on the windowsill ripen<br />on their own schedule,<br />and I have never once rushed them.<br /><br />There are people I owe messages to.<br />I compose them in the shower.<br />They come out perfect there.<br />Warm water, no audience, no send button.<br />The words arrive whole<br />and I let them slide down the drain.<br />The tile doesn't need a reply.

## Life and the pursuit of happiness

Happiness isn't the destination<br />I've always circled on the map.<br />It's the side road I always take<br />because the main road feels too certain.<br /><br />I think I want it the way I want sleep:<br />badly, and only when I stop<br />trying hard enough.<br />The body knows. I'm still catching up.<br /><br />Life is mostly the in-between parts:<br />water boiling,<br />the load in the dryer,<br />the window I stare out of<br />while thinking about something else entirely.<br />And somehow, that's enough.<br />That's almost everything.

Maybe one day the shower version gets sent.<br />Maybe the drain is where good intentions go<br />to become something else:<br />sediment, or a sea, or silence.<br /><br />I read somewhere that summer is for rest.<br />I also read it's for momentum.<br />I read a lot of contradictory things<br />and underline all of them.<br /><br />The days are long.<br />I use them up slowly.<br />As if I'm rationing something<br />I don't realize is infinite.<br /><br />At night I sit outside just long enough<br />to remember I love being outside.<br />Then I go back in.<br /><br />I'm still figuring out what I want.<br />Summer doesn't seem bothered.<br />It keeps showing up,<br />hot and certain,<br />while I stare at it<br />trying to look like I belong to it.
