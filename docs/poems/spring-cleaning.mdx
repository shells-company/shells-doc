title: "Spring Cleaning"
---

The tears are pouring down.<br />The most rain I've seen in at least a year and a half. It rains all weekend. It only rains this month on weekends.<br />It must be spring.<br /><br />I'm inside. Just inside<br />trying not to catch cabin fever.<br />My body is tired and I'm lying down watching my plants begin to bloom<br />I contemplate<br />I get rid of the old<br />I take everything out<br />I think I have two years of back taxes<br />I hate thinking about it, so I lean on Uber Eats<br />my legs refuse to leave my doorstep.<br />I'm still at home, at square one.<br />I'm not moving<br />or am I moving in another direction<br />my ears devour books while my eyes struggle to keep up<br />it feels like a biohack, but it feels great<br />I absorb books better when I hear them than when I read them<br />I finished the book, time to read poems<br />am I stagnant?<br />or am I moving in another direction<br />a direction<br />toward art<br />is it too far?<br />is it too fast?<br />or slow?<br /><br />I read poetry,<br />trying to move forward<br />someone else's words<br />filling the gaps in mine<br />I read a line and it lands somewhere I didn't expect<br />in the middle of something I've never said out loud<br /><br />I feel like some adversary is still dragging me down<br />my former self<br />I have to fix what he has<br />just me, too busy to take care of myself, right?<br />and then what do I even want? why don't I know?<br /><br />I read another poem<br />someone figured it out<br />or at least wrote as if they figured it out<br />maybe that's enough<br />maybe that's the whole trick<br />write as if you figured it out<br />until you actually do<br /><br />I never talk to my former self.. I hear he writes a lot.<br />My whole past without lessons learned or discovered<br />I read some of it, but there are always gaps<br />and if I ever feel a gap in the writing<br />I'm just<br />in a different genre.<br />The documentation never stops<br />I hate looking back.<br />It scares me<br /><br />Too bad.<br /><br />My mirror refuses to look at me<br />I'm ashamed of myself.<br />The glass knows what I've been avoiding<br />it holds the face of every email I didn't read<br />every call I didn't answer<br />every promise I made to my old self<br />and buried under delivery receipts<br />and unfinished playlists<br /><br />I pass by it sideways<br />as if I can sneak past unseen<br />as if I'm a ghost in my own apartment<br />I'm the ghost that haunts me<br /><br />But the mirror doesn't forget<br />it just waits<br />still, patient<br />the way debt waits<br />the way voicemails wait<br />the way the person I was supposed to become waits<br />just,<br />waiting<br /><br />I don't hate what I see<br />but I don't know it<br />and that's worse somehow<br />a stranger wearing my face<br />living in my mess<br />watching my plants bloom<br />without me<br /><br />High, and doing my taxes, I look back.<br />A rare view<br />in my bed, with all the aches<br />I record myself, read poetry<br />I talk to this page<br />I never stop writing.<br />I never stop documenting<br />and as I record this spoken poem<br />I find fragments from earlier recordings<br />these recordings, me listing all the things<br />that "will fix me."<br />I line them all up on the bed<br />each task will only fix a part of me<br />but a part of me is not me<br />and I'm still broken<br />what if I fix all the broken things<br />the list gets longer<br /><br />The list keeps growing<br />I get frustrated<br />I walk to my couch<br />I lie on it and think<br />if these are all top priorities, why am I on the couch<br />talking about them<br />I didn't choose any of those options<br />and I chose to talk<br />to complain to myself<br />the person who doesn't listen to himself complaining<br />just complains<br />Now I'm listening. I'm finally listening while doing my taxes.<br />But I have to record<br />so I don't miss a second of myself.<br /><br />I need W2 forms and income records<br />I feel nauseous, I ask the assistant to search my messages.<br />All the unread, that "I'll get to soon."<br />I avoided a painful memory, I'm just moving forward.<br /><br />I talk more about how I feel<br />dizzy, I spill more secrets,<br />whisper them to my toilet<br />the food must have been bad.<br />Uber says I ordered this in October, I don't remember.<br />More secrets pour out<br />I always said I wanted to eat here.<br />did I spill secrets last time?<br /><br />Turns out I filed my taxes last year. And the year before<br />I'm fully up to date on my taxes.<br />And I got a refund<br />I just forgot I paid them<br />I didn't listen to myself.<br />A random fragment of time, and I bet it's recorded too.<br /><br />Still tagged as unread.<br /><br />I didn't cross anything off my list.<br />Out of everything on the list.<br />None of it included my taxes.<br />That would have required looking at unread messages<br />and I somehow avoided it.<br />But I finished my taxes<br /><br />It felt great to flush the toilet<br />I never knew a toilet could be such a great therapist
