I feel like my world is exploding.

I hate it when the world explodes.<br />Graduation day.<br />All the fragments of the life I knew are gone.

Today feels like this,<br />my friends are gone.<br />Fragments scattering to the different edges of space.<br />Do I cling to a fragment?<br />Or do I go to my own place?

I am always "the last to leave"<br />or the first of them.<br />But is that always true?

If you leave someone, you'll always feel like you're the first.<br />And if they need your goodbye, you're the last.

Do you just want more time?

They haven't entirely gone, but they've gone from the "me" that was always a work in progress.

That is the "me" who needed them.<br />And now all that remains is —<br />"may it not be an empty promise."
