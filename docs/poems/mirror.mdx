---
title: "mirror"
---

The man in the mirror waves at me. His features grow clearer.<br />Wiping the glasses as a metaphor for more reflection.<br />I hang up my clothes.<br />I put them on a rack that lets me see everything that makes me.

> I should use these mirrors..

I can sit on the bus.<br />I don't always have to be the one suffering most.<br />I can rest.

I used to fear bees.<br />But they won't hurt me.<br />They just want to get back to their queen.<br />Just like me.<br />Longing to be...

The bee doesn't question the hive.<br />It knows its wings, its weight, its way.<br />It carries what it finds and flies it home.<br />No hesitation. No delay.<br />I want to move like that.<br />With purpose. Humming softly.<br />Knowing that the sweetness I've gathered<br />is enough to bring back.<br />The queen doesn't wait to judge me.<br />She waits because she needs me.<br />And maybe I need that too,<br />a hive to return to.<br />A place that hums when I enter.

I comb my hair more, I groom myself

I like how I look.<br />I've gotten older.<br />I care about how I look.

The man in the mirror stands taller now.<br />I didn't notice.<br />Did the mirror correct my posture?

Okay, that's enough cleaning.<br />If I cleaned everything, what would I do with my free time?

A passing note:

> Maybe this is the question I was afraid to ask.<br />Not _how do I fix myself?_<br />But _what do I do when I'm not broken?_
>
> I've never just sat in a clean room and... simply been.<br />I always had a pile to point to.<br />A reason to stay busy.<br />Mess meant I wasn't ready yet.<br />A reason to be "bad"
